## OpenBack v0.35.1 - Roblox Solo Conquest Prototype

- Start a native solo conquest prototype against a deterministic opponent on a newly authored river-crossing board. Click or tap neighbouring land to expand.
- Troops and gold grow on the server. Orders spend troops; invalid orders, remote captures and rapid request floods are rejected. Capture the opponent's final territory to win, or compete for the most land at ten minutes.
- Switch overhead/3D views during play, replay a finished match, or return to the unit showcase. Match controls stay inside Roblox's device-safe area.
- This is an early gameplay prototype, not the complete OpenBack port. Multiplayer, construction, naval combat, weapons, the full tutorial and Robux purchases are not yet available. The regular OpenBack game is unchanged.

## OpenBack v0.35.0 - Roblox Foundation Preview

- The Roblox-only foundation introduces ten original unit silhouettes on a newly authored island showcase, with overhead and angled 3D camera views of the same scene.
- A safe-area-aware preview header provides a view switch and clearly identifies this as a presentation preview, not a playable match. No Robux purchases or subscriptions are active.
- View controls recover when Roblox replaces the camera, and visibly wait if a camera is temporarily unavailable.
- Camera framing leaves space below the preview header and starts farther out on portrait screens so the unit showcase is easier to see.
- PC players receive mouse controls; touch players receive larger buttons, two-finger orbit and pinch zoom. Narrow screens stack controls, and the preview adapts when input or screen orientation changes.
- The normal OpenBack game is unchanged. Combat, multiplayer matches, the full tutorial and public Roblox launch remain in development.

Created by **frootz jhklphy**.
